I began a graveyard shift job in 2019 for Covenant Health in their data
center. I showed a curiosity in learning more, so a dayshift mentor
emerged. It started with a Raspberry Pi and a dream, but it quickly took
on a life of its own.

During COVID-19, there was a huge surge in creating home labs and home
offices, so there was an abundance of up-to-date content to watch and
read. What started as a single Raspberry Pi grew into a home lab
infrastructure that, at its peak, included 32 computers running 17
different operating systems and 6 external devices.

Using the home lab, Alex taught himself Linux, basic networking, Docker,
Kubernetes, Ansible, and much more. During this talk, he will discuss
his journey, his successes and failures, and the decisions---both good
and bad---that shaped his path, with the hope of encouraging others to
embark on similar learning adventures.
