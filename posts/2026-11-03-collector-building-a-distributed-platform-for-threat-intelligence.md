Breach and malware-derived datasets can reveal interesting relationships between identities, accounts domains, and threat actors that are valuable for threat intelligence and security investigations. In this talk, I’ll cover Collector, the platform I built to acquire, catalog, and deduplicate these datasets from Telegram; the engineering challenges that pushed it from a simple downloader into a distributed system; and a few things I learned from analyzing the data.
