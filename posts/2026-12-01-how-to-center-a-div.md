Learn the different ways to center a div, understand what a div actually is, and discover common mistakes to avoid. This talk goes beyond basic centering to give you a deeper understanding of how CSS layout works. Having done my fair share of debugging CSS and aligning elements, I know that even senior developers need a refresher sometimes. Today, you can easily ask AI to write your layout code, but it’s important to know how to fix it when AI gets it wrong.
